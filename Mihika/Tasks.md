# TASK PLANNING

This document outlines the key project tasks, expected timeline, and weekly responsibilities. It will be updated as the project progresses and responsibilities become more clearly defined.

# Overall Timeline

## Next Few Weeks

* **Establish benchmark approaches**

  * Identify benchmark models for both path-signature modeling and anomaly detection.
  * Review benchmarks used in the reference paper and related literature.
  * Identify additional baseline methods for comparison.

* **Generate and evaluate path signatures**

  * Implement path-signature feature generation for the selected market data.
  * Experiment with different signature configurations and model specifications.
  * Evaluate whether path-signature features improve performance relative to benchmark representations.

* **Research anomaly-detection methods**

  * Review anomaly- and novelty-detection approaches applicable to financial time series and path-space data.
  * Determine which methods are most appropriate for the project's market-event and regime-detection objectives.

## Later Stages

* **Implement anomaly-detection models**

  * Apply selected anomaly-detection methods using path-signature features.
  * Tune and compare alternative modeling approaches.

* **Evaluate model performance**

  * Test whether anomaly scores successfully identify known periods of abnormal market behavior.
  * Compare results across benchmark and path-signature-based approaches.
  * Evaluate economic significance, including potential P&L implications of signals generated by the models.

# WEEKLY PLAN

## 08/31 – 09/04 | Assigned to Everyone

### Data Collection

* Identify and obtain suitable high-frequency / tick-level market data.
* Understand the structure, frequency, coverage, and limitations of the available datasets.

### Benchmark Research

* Determine appropriate benchmark approaches for:

  * Path-signature modeling.
  * Anomaly / novelty detection.
* Review benchmarks used in the reference paper.
* Research additional potential baseline methods.
* Investigate simulation-based benchmarks, such as anomaly detection relative to paths generated from an SDE / Monte Carlo model.

### Paper & Methodology

* Develop a strong understanding of the reference paper.
* Understand:

  * What path signatures represent.
  * How signature features are constructed.
  * How the paper performs novelty / anomaly detection.
  * Which components of the methodology can be adapted to financial market data.
* Clarify the project's initial modeling pipeline and research questions.

### Project Infrastructure

* Set up and organize the GitHub repository.
* Establish documentation standards and repository structure.
* Document relevant papers, datasets, methodology decisions, and initial experiments.
